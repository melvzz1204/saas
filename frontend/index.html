    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is there a 1-year promo?

    Yes. Yearly is ₱25,000 per branch instead of ₱36,000 — you save ₱11,000 (about ₱2,083/mo, 2+ months free). Same full-access plan, price-locked for 12 months, one invoice per year. You can switch monthly ↔ yearly anytime from My Subscription.

    How long does clinic approval take?

    Once you register with your business license and medical license documents, our team reviews the application and usually approves it within 24 hours.

    What's included in the ₱3,000/month plan?

    Everything. The live appointment board, online booking, patient records, staff logins, your public clinic page, pricing lists, and reviews no per-seat fees or add-on charges.

    Do patients need to install anything?

    No. Patients book through your public clinic page in any browser. Your team logs in from a desktop, tablet, or phone.

    Can I run multiple branches?

    Yes. Each branch is its own workspace with its own staff, hours, and price list, billed at ₱3,000 per branch per month.

    Is patient data secure?

    Patient records are isolated per clinic, access is role-based and PIN/password protected, and data is encrypted in transit and at rest with daily backups.

    What happens after I register?

    You'll sign in as the clinic administrator, complete your profile and hours, add your staff and treatment prices, then share your clinic page with patients.
