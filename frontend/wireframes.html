Conversion & UX annotations

1. Value prop above the fold H1 + subhead + product mockup + live clinic count — no carousel, no hero image downloads.
2. One primary CTA per viewport "Get your clinic onboarded" leads to the registration flow; secondary actions are clearly de-emphasized.
3. Authentic social proof Real, API-driven clinic directory + live count — replaces fabricated star ratings and fake quotes from the previous design.
4. Objection handling Pricing is one flat card; FAQ sits directly after testimonials to catch late-stage objections; security badges anchor the pricing section.
5. Persistent navigation Sticky header with sign-in + CTA at every scroll position; hamburger exposes the same links on mobile.
6. Accessibility by default Skip link, landmarks, 44px targets, AA contrast, native <details> FAQ, and reduced-motion fallbacks throughout.
